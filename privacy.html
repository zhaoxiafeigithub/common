Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afteryears handles information when you use the app.

1. Data Collection

Afteryears is designed to operate primarily on-device. The app does not require you to create an account to play.

Game progress, choices, character state, and related save data may be stored locally on your device so that you can continue your game later.

2. Personal Information

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your real name, home address, contacts, photos, or precise location as part of normal gameplay.

3. Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by the relevant app store platform, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud save, or other third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices before or when those services are introduced.

5. Children's Privacy

Afteryears is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in error, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device unless you delete it through the app, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove the relevant local storage.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact
